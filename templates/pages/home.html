SHARE WHAT YOU KNOW

Your experience could
inspire a classroom.

Offer a guest lesson in your subject or profession. The association team can review your offer, coordinate with the school, and arrange a suitable session.

Offer to teach ↗
  1. 01

    Tell us your subject

    Share your experience and the students you would like to help.

  2. 02

    Plan with the team

    A coordinator reviews your offer and agrees a time with the school.

  3. 03

    Make the session count

    After the activity, the team can record attendance and what students gained.

SHOW WHAT IS POSSIBLE

A conversation can
change a direction.

Lead a career conversation, a practical skills workshop, or a seminar that helps students see their next step.

Offer a seminar ↗
  1. 01

    Suggest a useful topic

    Careers, higher education, digital skills, and everyday learning all have a place.

  2. 02

    Agree the details

    Set a date, audience, speaker, and venue with the coordinating team.

  3. 03

    Share the learning

    Publish the programme and record a useful summary after it takes place.
